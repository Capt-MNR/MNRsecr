---
name: Mobile two-surface architecture
description: The mobile companion has a lightweight quick surface and a full records surface in one app.
---

The mobile product should stay as one app with two directly switchable surfaces: a low-friction quick secretary for short requests, replies, and approvals; and a records surface for the user's structured data and history. Native push and background actions should extend the quick surface rather than create a third workflow.

**Why:** The product needs the speed of a notification-oriented assistant without hiding the user's durable records behind the web dashboard.

**How to apply:** Keep the quick surface one tap away from records, reuse the existing tenant-scoped APIs, and add deeper record editing as a separate flow without turning the mobile app into a heavy dashboard.
