---
name: Mobile two-surface architecture
description: The mobile app opens on a lightweight quick surface and contains a progressive secretary office.
---

The mobile app should stay as one product with two directly switchable surfaces: a lightweight quick secretary that opens by default for short requests, replies, notifications, and approvals; and a main Personal Secretary Office for attention, today context, recent activity, financial orientation, people/projects, and discovery. The full records browser is a separate destination from the office. The web app remains available as the broader desktop workspace, but mobile is not just a quick-only companion.

**Why:** The user wants mobile to feel fast at launch while the main surface feels like a calm office rather than a larger chat or an ERP-style records table.

**How to apply:** Default to quick, keep a one-tap office entry, use bounded today context for the office, defer full records/financial loading until explicit exploration, reuse tenant-scoped APIs, and reserve deeper editing for a follow-up without removing the main mobile surface.
